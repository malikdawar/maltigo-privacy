MaltiGo: Delete your account and data

This page explains how to delete your MaltiGo account and the data linked to it.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open MaltiGo and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Can't open the app?

Email malikdawar.ams@gmail.com from the address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my MaltiGo account". We will delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your email address, display name and password (held by Firebase Authentication).
  • Your cloud-backed-up learning progress (held in Firebase Realtime Database).

What is kept

Nothing linked to your account is kept after deletion. Progress stored only on your device is removed when you delete the account in the app or uninstall the app.

Delete only your progress

To clear your learning progress but keep your account, go to Profile → Settings → Reset progress.
