MaltiGo Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

MaltiGo helps you learn Maltese. This policy explains what the app stores and why.

1. Data kept on your device

Your learning progress (XP, streaks, completed lessons, stars, mistakes, settings and reminder time) is stored on your device. The app works offline, and you can use most of it as a guest without an account.

2. Account data (optional)

If you create an account, your email address, display name and a securely hashed password are handled by Firebase Authentication (a Google service) so you can sign in and reset your password. If you continue as a guest, no account data is collected.

3. Cloud backup (signed-in users only)

When you are signed in, your learning progress is backed up to Firebase Realtime Database (a Google service), linked to your account, so you can restore it on another device. Guests are never backed up.

4. What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show ads, use analytics or advertising trackers, or track your location. The app does not access your contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

5. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device at the time you choose. No personal data is sent to a server to deliver them.

6. Children

The app is suitable for learners of all ages. Children should use account features only with the permission of a parent or guardian. Guest mode collects no personal information.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off reminders any time in Settings.
  • Reset your progress in Profile → Settings → Reset progress.
  • Delete your account and the data stored for it (including the cloud backup) in Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your device.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by Firebase's security. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
